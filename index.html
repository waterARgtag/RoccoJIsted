Meet Rocco

Curious mind.
Camera-ready
personality.

Rocco J Isted is a young content creator with a love of gaming, technology, travel and Formula One. Comfortable on camera and full of questions, he brings his own perspective to products, places and the people he meets.

His campaign experience includes CeX, Disney, LEGO, Cadbury and Shop Simon, alongside family experiences and adventure parks in the UK and USA. From gaming content to on-location storytelling, the focus is simple: make it engaging, relatable and fun.
